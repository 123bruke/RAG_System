ACME Store - Customer Support Policy

Support hours are 9:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday. Messages sent outside these hours are answered on the next business day. We are closed on public holidays.

Our first response time target is 24 hours for email and 5 minutes for live chat during support hours. Urgent issues such as payment errors or account lockouts are prioritised.

All hardware products include a warranty of 12 months from the date of purchase. The warranty covers manufacturing defects but does not cover accidental damage, water damage or unauthorised repairs.

To make a warranty claim, contact support with your order number and a description of the problem. Our team will either repair the product, replace it, or issue a refund under the refund policy.

Customers can change or cancel an order within 2 hours of placing it, before the order is handed to the carrier. After that, the order must be returned using the standard return process.

Escalations: if you are not satisfied with the first answer, ask for a supervisor. A supervisor will review your case and reply within 2 business days.
