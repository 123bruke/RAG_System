ACME Store - Refund Policy

Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. To be eligible, the product must be unused and returned in its original packaging together with the receipt or order number.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. After we receive the returned item, the refund is processed within 5 to 7 business days. Your bank may need additional time before the money appears on your statement.

Digital products and gift cards are non-refundable once the license key or gift code has been delivered. If a digital product does not work as described, contact support and we will help you troubleshoot or replace it.

Items that arrive damaged can be replaced or refunded. To claim a damaged item, contact support with photos of the item and the packaging within 14 days of delivery.

Return shipping costs are paid by the customer unless the item was defective or the wrong item was sent. In those cases ACME Store provides a prepaid return label.

Sale items marked "final sale" cannot be returned or refunded. Orders placed with a discount code follow the same 30 day refund period, but the refund amount equals the price actually paid.
